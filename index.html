Daily Bread Circle — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Daily Bread Circle ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the Daily Bread Circle mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and basic profile details (such as display name) to authenticate and manage your account.
  • User-Generated Content: Prayer requests, reflections, comments, and interactions that you voluntarily post within the community.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Basic authentication identifiers needed to keep your session secure and deliver app functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide and maintain Daily Bread Circle—including authenticating your login, displaying community prayer requests and devotionals, and keeping the community safe. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your account and community data are securely hosted using Supabase with encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and row-level access controls.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can edit or delete your own posts directly in the app. You may also delete your account and associated personal data at any time through the app's profile/settings menu or by contacting us at the email below.

5. Children's Privacy

Daily Bread Circle is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
